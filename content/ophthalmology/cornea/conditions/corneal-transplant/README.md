# Corneal transplant

Documents for corneal transplant (keratoplasty): deep anterior lamellar keratoplasty (DALK), penetrating keratoplasty (PK) and endothelial keratoplasty (DSEK, DMEK), and their aftercare.

Risk and outcome figures live in `evidence/uk/ophthalmology/cornea/risks.yaml` under `dalk`, `pk` and `ek`.
